## Danish WWTP Interaction Network
**"A Multilayered Network Reveals the Centrality of Newly Discovered Nucleocytoviricota, Virophages, and Polinton-like Viruses"**.

This is the github repository to the publication 

### Abstract
>Viruses of the phylum Nucleocytoviricota are paradigm-shifting entities due to their exceptionally large genomes and complex gene repertoires, which blur the lines between viral and cellular life. Previous research has leveraged computational approaches to map their extensive diversity, while experimental work has started to elucidate the intricate networks they form with hosts, symbionts, sympatric bacteria and co-infecting virophages and other mobile genetic elements. Here, we analysed deeply sequenced metagenomes sampled from wastewater treatment plants in Denmark, an environment with rapid abiotic changes and known to be a hotbed of dense microbial communities. We discovered 62 novel nucleocytoviruses together with 15 virophages and 14 polinton-like viruses. By integrating them with potential hosts and symbionts into a multilayered interaction network, we explore and elucidate the role of these entities on a mesocosm scale. We demonstrate the centrality of nucleocytoviruses in this engineered ecosystem, positioning them as important players that influence microbial community structure and evolution.
